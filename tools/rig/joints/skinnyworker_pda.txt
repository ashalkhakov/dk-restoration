# The skinny worker's PDA, for tools/rig/iqm_hold.py (dk: hold=this file).
# Daikatana's was a folded piece of his mesh at his chest (SkinnySkinboy
# 451-464) and a translucent box (trans33); ours is a clamshell of its own
# (characters/props/pda*.iqm, tools/models/make_pda.py), in his right hand.
#
#   hold <joint> <bone> <model>          the joint in his hand, the model on it
#   grip <anim> <surface>                held where Daikatana's surface sat in
#                                        the middle of anim, its face to his eyes
#   lid <joint> <model> <degrees>        the lid's joint, on the hold joint's
#                                        origin (the hinge), turned so far about
#                                        its y folded
#   drop <mesh>[:first-last]             Daikatana's out of his mesh
#   show <anim> <first> <last|end> <open at first> <open at last>
#                                        the frames it's out (0 from the first),
#                                        how open (0 folded, 1 open) between
#
# faaeaa: his hand at his pocket by frame 4, the PDA in it from the start,
# folded, opening as he lifts it (6 to 9); eambd: he makes his notes, the
# beeps; eaafaa: folding as his hand goes down (8 to 12), put away by its
# last frame.

hold pda Rhand characters/props/pda.iqm
grip eambd trans33
lid pda_lid characters/props/pda_lid.iqm 180
drop SkinnySkinboy:451-464
drop trans33

show faaeaa 0 5 0 0
show faaeaa 6 9 0 1
show faaeaa 10 end 1 1
show eambd 0 end 1 1
show eaafaa 0 7 1 1
show eaafaa 8 12 1 0
show eaafaa 13 16 0 0
